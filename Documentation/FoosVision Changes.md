# FoosVision 1.1.0                   2026-

## Supported Platforms
- Android 13+

## New
- Start and stop Training from the viewer after table setup. Practice Snake, Pull, and Tick-Tack Pass on either side of the table with spoken recorder cues and configurable waits and variant weights.
- Review recognized training shots and passes in quarter-speed replay. The clip repeats until the next preparation is recognized and at least one loop has played.

## Improved
- Documented local network and port requirements for recorder-viewer communication.
- Improved recorder discovery reliability by waiting longer for UDP announcements, limiting TCP fallback probing to foreground use, and avoiding interrupted fallback probe runs.

## Fixed
- Fixed an error that could prevent a replay from being created while recording continued.

## Changed
- Renamed the Android launcher icons to `Foos Rec` and `Foos View`.
- Renamed the pre-game flow to setup.
- Renamed the viewer tracking FPS readout to process FPS and show it during setup and game sessions.
